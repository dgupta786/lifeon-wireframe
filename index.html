Project Build

Today, our church gathers and carries out its ministries from rented spaces. While we are grateful for every place God has provided for us, rented spaces come with uncertainty. Rent, availability, and other circumstances can change over time, making it difficult to plan and build for the future with confidence.

As our church continues to grow, so does the need for a permanent place, a building where we can gather, worship, grow together, serve one another, and continue the work God has called us to do.

That is the heart behind Project Build.

LifeON is a venture to raise support for Project Build: a permanent building for our church.

Why Project Build?

A church is not defined by a building. The Church is God's people.

But a permanent building can provide a foundation from which the Church can continue to serve, grow, and reach people for generations to come.

Having our own place would give us greater stability and the freedom to pursue ministries and initiatives that may be difficult to fully develop while operating from rented spaces.

We dream of a place where:

  • 01People can gather and worship God freely.
  • 02Lives can be transformed through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
  • 03Believers can grow in faith and discipleship.
  • 04Children, young people, and families can be nurtured and strengthened.
  • 05Ministries can grow and serve the needs of people.
  • 06Future generations can continue the work of God's Kingdom.

Project Build is our step of faith towards making that vision possible.

Our Mission

To unite our hearts, hands, gifts, and resources to help build a permanent home for our church, a place that will serve Christ, strengthen His people, and enable us to continue doing God's work for generations to come.

Our Vision

To see a Christ-centred and God-glorifying home established for our church, where generations of people can gather, grow in faith, serve one another, encounter God's love, and be equipped to reach others.

Our Goal

To raise meaningful and sustainable financial support for Project Build by pursuing opportunities and initiatives that enable us to contribute towards building a permanent home for our church.

Let's Build Together.

Not for our own name.
Not merely for a building.
But for the glory of God and the work of His Kingdom.

“Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labor in vain.”

Psalm 127:1

We will work faithfully, give generously, plan wisely, and build together.
But above all, we depend on God.

Because ultimately, unless the Lord builds the house, our efforts are in vain.
